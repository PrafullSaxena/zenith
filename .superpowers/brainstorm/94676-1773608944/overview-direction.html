C: Bento Grid

Apple-inspired bento layout with varying card sizes. Dense information display, gradient hero banner, compact entity grid. Maximum info density with visual hierarchy.

My Recommendation: A + C hybrid

  • Glass cards from A for stats (premium feel)
  • Bento grid layout from C (information density)
  • Donut chart from A for languages
  • Spring animations on all cards (staggered entrance)

Why not B?

  • Health score requires subjective metrics we don't have
  • Hero element takes too much vertical space
  • Less information density than A or C
